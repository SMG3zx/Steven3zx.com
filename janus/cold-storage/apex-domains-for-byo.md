# Apex (bare) domains for BYO domains

Parked on 2026-10-19 from the Janus platform map (Linear ENG-44, bring-your-own domain ticket ENG-50).

A CNAME cannot sit at a bare apex such as `example.com` in standard DNS, so BYO domains support subdomains only. Apex support needs a paid Cloudflare add-on and a different DNS setup for the customer.

**Brings it back:** paid revenue that covers the Cloudflare add-on cost, or repeated customer requests for apex domains.
